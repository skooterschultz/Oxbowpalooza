Pick your own Ozark adventure

Things to Do

Roaring River

  • Trout fishing and a visit to the fish hatchery.
  • Seven hiking trails, picnic areas, playgrounds, and the nature center.
  • The park lodge has a full-service restaurant.

At the Resort

  • Pool, splash pad, playground, and basketball court.
  • Horseshoes, cornhole, fire rings, picnic tables, and plenty of visiting space.
  • Family games and activities are still being planned.

A comfortable week by the river

What to Bring

  • A reusable water bottle and a favorite drink cup.
  • Comfortable walking shoes, river shoes, and clothes you can layer.
  • Sun protection, insect repellent, and light rain protection.
  • A camp chair or picnic blanket for visiting outdoors.
  • Fishing gear and any permits you need if you plan to fish.
  • Family photos, stories, recipes, or keepsakes you would enjoy sharing.

Fishing at Roaring River requires a Missouri fishing permit unless exempt, plus a daily trout tag during the summer season. Check current rules and prices before the trip. View official fishing information
